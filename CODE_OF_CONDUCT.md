# Community conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss technical claims using reproducible examples and evidence. Give useful credit, respect privacy and maintain the project's authorized-use scope.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, deliberate disclosure of private information and attempts to involve the community in unauthorized activity are not acceptable. TabGrinder may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.

For concerns, request a private contact method from the maintainer without publishing personal information. The maintainer will assess concerns using available evidence; this document does not promise a particular response time or outcome.
